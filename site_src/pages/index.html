Four categories, one image at a time

The classifier looks at a single 2D slice and returns a probability for each category.

Glioma

arises from glial cells

Infiltrative tumors of the brain's supporting tissue, from slow-growing to highly aggressive.

Meningioma

arises from the meninges

Usually slow-growing tumors attached to the membranes around the brain.

Pituitary macroadenoma

arises from the pituitary gland

Tumors larger than 10 mm at the base of the brain, often pressing on nearby structures.

Healthy

no visible tumor

Normal slices, included so the model also learns what a normal brain looks like.

The number we first saw was wrong

During training, validation accuracy read 100%. On truly held-out images the better model scores 86.9%, and the original one 80.4%. Neighbouring slices from the same scan likely sat on both sides of the split. Read what happened and how to fix it.

Research project, not medical advice

NeuroScan cannot diagnose, treat or rule out any condition. A brain MRI needs review by a qualified radiologist or physician. If you are worried about symptoms, see a doctor.
