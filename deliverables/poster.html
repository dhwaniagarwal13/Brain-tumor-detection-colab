The problem

Different brain tumors need different treatment, and the type is judged from MRI. This project trains a model to sort a slice into Glioma, Meningioma, Pituitary Macroadenoma or Healthy, as a decision-support demo.

The data

Multi-Class Brain Tumor MRI Dataset (Jamil and Khan, 2025, Mendeley Data V2, CC BY 4.0), split 80/20 and balanced:

  • Training: 908 images (236 / 232 / 221 / 219)
  • Testing: 229 images, untouched until final scoring

The method

1PrepareResize to 224 x 224, three channels
2AugmentFlip, rotate, zoom, contrast, brightness
3Train headMobileNetV2 frozen, new classifier, 8 epochs
4Fine-tuneUnfreeze top layers, low learning rate, 25 epochs
5EvaluateHeld-out test set: F1, AUC, confusion matrix

What I built

Limits and next steps

  • Small dataset; validation by slice leaks, so validation reads 100% while test is 86.9%
  • Next: patient-level split, EfficientNetV2, ensembling, in-browser TensorFlow.js
